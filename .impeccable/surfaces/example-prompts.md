# Shared example prompt cards

Approved refinement: make prompts visually distinct, copyable or inspirational, and always precede their associated diagrams. Preserve the main evergreen identity and the microsite's separate palette.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A brief to pick up and adapt, not terminal output.
OWN-WORLD: Pale citron and evergreen on the main site; lilac and violet in the AWS studio. Prose, a divided toolbar and a clearly labelled copy action.
STORY: Read a short brief, copy or expand it, then inspect the resulting diagram.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 1 · Prompt, two-line preview, copy button and native disclosure; 2 · Diagram follows. The full prompt remains the copy source while collapsed.
FORM: Extend the established system under the user's explicit prompt-before-diagram direction; no replacement-world selection.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

No prompt provenance is strengthened. Existing authoring text remains intact. Briefs are not deterministic generation evidence. Native disclosures work without JavaScript; denied clipboard access opens and selects the complete text.
