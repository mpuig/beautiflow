Architecture assumptions & omissions
  • The upload broker derives allowed tenant/object keys from verified identity and issues bounded temporary capabilities. The browser uploads; the identity arrow does not mean Lambda streams the audio.
  • Cross-account workflow and MediaConvert roles need explicit source, destination and KMS policies. Job completion and human rights approval are separate preconditions; neither S3 presence nor successful encoding grants publication authority.
  • Publication copies approved objects and updates the catalogue with reconciliation for partial failures. CloudFront origin access control authorizes private origin reads; signed viewer URLs separately authorize listeners.
  • Master replication assumes source/destination versioning, replication roles, KMS grants and deliberate delete-marker/retention policies. It is asynchronous and is not a substitute for protected backups. No real music or rights claims are made.
