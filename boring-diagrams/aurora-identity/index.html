Grand Aurora: Tenant Isolation

Art Deco · focused identity view

One issuer, two application accounts, and independent authorization at each cell. The pooled path binds a validated tenant to a transaction; the dedicated path also checks its cell allowlist.

Expand Grand Aurora identity/isolation. Architecture assumptions & omissions
  • Identity lines show browser-carried credentials, not Cognito initiating calls into the APIs. Each API validates issuer, audience, expiry and scopes. VPC Link integrations and internal ALBs remain in their own account/region.
  • The app derives the subject from verified identity, checks selected-hotel membership and assigned-cell policy, and sets tenant context locally within a transaction. A client-supplied tenant ID is a selection, not evidence of entitlement.
  • Pooled PostgreSQL isolation assumes non-owner/non-BYPASSRLS roles, forced row policies, safe transaction pooling and tests covering raw SQL, background workers and administrative paths. The picture does not prove those controls.
  • Separate enterprise credentials/database/account reduce shared-data-plane exposure; the app still rejects other tenants. Identity federation, revocation, privileged operations and complete networking are not implementation artifacts here. This is a focused authored view of the overview’s cell aggregates.
