The design brief for this example.

Design a fictional private ML review platform with separate research and production accounts, versioned artifacts, human-approved model promotion, private inference access, a review service across two AZs, authenticated reviewers and a workflow-gated accepted catalogue. %% Fictional private ML review. All predictions require human acceptance before publication.
%% @boundary research account
%% @boundary researchRegion region
%% @boundary production account
%% @boundary primary region
%% @boundary privateVpc vpc
%% @boundary azA az
%% @boundary azB az
%% @boundary accepted logical
group research(cloud)[Account: Research training]
group researchRegion(cloud)[Region: Primary] in research
group production(cloud)[Account: Survey production]
group primary(cloud)[Region: Primary] in production
group privateVpc(cloud)[VPC: Private review service] in primary
group azA(cloud)[AZ: A - private tasks] in privateVpc
group azB(cloud)[AZ: B - private tasks] in privateVpc
group accepted(cloud)[Logical: Accepted evidence] in primary
service training(aws:sagemaker)[Model training job] in researchRegion
service models(aws:s3)[Versioned model artifacts] in researchRegion
service promotion(aws:step-functions)[Approved model promotion] in researchRegion
service endpoint(aws:sagemaker)[Private inference endpoint] in primary
service raw(aws:s3)[Raw survey photographs] in primary
service workflow(aws:step-functions)[Survey review workflow] in primary
service reviewA(aws:fargate)[Review app tasks A] in azA
service reviewB(aws:fargate)[Review app tasks B] in azB
service auth(aws:cognito)[Researcher sign-in] in primary
service alb(aws:elb)[Internal review ALB] in privateVpc
service publish(aws:lambda)[Publish accepted result] in accepted
service records(aws:dynamodb)[Reviewed catalogue] in accepted
%% @edge training models request | Training role writes versioned model artifacts; accuracy is not established by this diagram.
training:R --> L:models
%% @edge models promotion control | Human-approved version is selected for promotion; S3 presence alone is not approval.
models:R --> L:promotion
%% @edge promotion endpoint control | Approved deployment assumes a scoped cross-account role and explicit artifact/key permissions.
promotion:B --> T:endpoint
%% @edge raw workflow event | Configured S3 EventBridge rule starts execution; EventBridge node is omitted in this focused deployment view.
raw:T --> L:workflow
%% @edge workflow endpoint request | Workflow invokes the custom-trained endpoint through its execution role; VPC endpoints constrain service access.
workflow:R --> B:endpoint
%% @edge auth alb identity | Browser or corporate network presents identity to the review application; no public ALB is implied.
auth:R --> L:alb
%% @edge alb reviewA request | Private staff access reaches review tasks in AZ A through authorized network connectivity.
alb:R --> L:reviewA
%% @edge alb reviewB request | Independent task placement in AZ B; user identity is verified by the application.
alb:B --> L:reviewB
%% @edge reviewA workflow control | Approved reviewer action uses an authenticated backend and task-token callback; token never becomes public authority.
reviewA:R --> T:workflow
%% @edge reviewB workflow control | Rejected results do not advance to publication; review policy and audit are required.
reviewB:R --> R:workflow
%% @edge workflow publish control | Only the successful human-approval branch invokes publication.
workflow:B --> L:publish
%% @edge publish records request | Writes accepted labels with provenance; predictions and raw objects remain separate.
publish:R --> L:records

Architecture assumptions & omissions
  • Promotion assumes a reviewed artifact version, scoped cross-account deployment role and explicit bucket/key permissions. The diagram does not demonstrate model quality, safety or scientific validity.
  • SageMaker is shown as a regional managed service. Private inference access requires appropriate VPC endpoints and IAM/network configuration; training/inference infrastructure is not placed inside the review app’s AZ boxes.
  • The internal ALB is reachable only through assumed corporate/private connectivity. App-level identity verification and reviewer authorization remain necessary. Task-token callbacks go through an authenticated backend.
  • Only the successful approval branch publishes accepted records. Training data, predictions and accepted evidence have different retention/access policies. Training pipelines, endpoint HA settings, staff connectivity and complete audit infrastructure are omitted.
