The design brief for this example.

Design a fictional flash-sale ticketing platform with CloudFront/WAF, Cognito, JWT API, transactional reservation and outbox, stream-driven independent consumers, separate audit account and asynchronous read-only recovery replica. Show one authoritative writer region; do not claim globally consistent active-active inventory.

%% @boundary prod account
%% @boundary primary region
%% @boundary projections logical
%% @boundary recovery region
%% @boundary security account
%% @boundary auditRegion region
group prod(cloud)[Account: Ticket production]
group primary(cloud)[Region: Authoritative sales] in prod
group projections(cloud)[Logical: Async consumers] in primary
group recovery(cloud)[Region: Read-only recovery] in prod
group security(cloud)[Account: Security]
group auditRegion(cloud)[Region: Audit archive] in security
service edge(aws:cloudfront)[Protected ticket site] in prod
service waf(aws:waf)[Edge WAF policy] in prod
service auth(aws:cognito)[Ticket buyer sign-in] in primary
service api(aws:api-gateway)[JWT ticket API] in primary
service reserve(aws:lambda)[Reserve capacity] in primary
service sales(aws:dynamodb)[Reservations and outbox] in primary
service pipe(aws:eventbridge)[Streams to event bus] in primary
service bus(aws:eventbridge)[Committed purchases] in primary
service door(aws:lambda)[Door-list projector] in projections
service mail(aws:sqs)[Ticket delivery queue] in projections
service failed(aws:sqs)[Failed ticket delivery] in projections
service replica(aws:dynamodb)[Read-only sales replica] in recovery
service audit(aws:s3)[Protected audit archive] in auditRegion

waf:R --> L:edge

edge:R --> L:auth

auth:R --> L:api
%% @edge api reserve request | Authenticated booking command; server validates event, buyer and purchase limits.
api:R --> L:reserve
%% @edge reserve sales request | Transactional conditional capacity update and outbox record; application expiry, not TTL deletion, releases holds.
reserve:R --> L:sales
%% @edge sales pipe event | DynamoDB Streams feed a configured EventBridge Pipe with duplicate-tolerant processing.
sales:T --> B:pipe
%% @edge pipe bus event | Filter committed purchases; do not independently dual-write from reservation code.
pipe:R --> L:bus
%% @edge bus door event | Door-list projection can lag and must deduplicate event versions.
bus:R --> L:door
%% @edge bus mail event | Independent delivery queue decouples email work from the reservation transaction.
bus:T --> B:mail
%% @edge mail failed event | Configured SQS redrive after failed consumer attempts; mail worker is omitted from this projection view.
mail:R --> L:failed
%% @edge sales replica replication | Asynchronous global-table replica is read-only by application policy; no active-active capacity allocation.
sales:B --> L:replica
%% @edge reserve audit control | Audit pipeline records sensitive booking actions in a separate account; transport and KMS policies require implementation.
reserve:T --> L:audit

Architecture assumptions & omissions
  • Reservation code must atomically enforce capacity, purchase idempotency and outbox insertion. Hold expiry is application logic; asynchronous DynamoDB TTL deletion is not a booking clock.
  • The Streams-to-bus element aggregates an EventBridge Pipe and its configured target. Consumers tolerate duplicate events, lag and version changes. The mail worker and connection details are omitted.
  • The illustrated global-table replica is read-only by application/IAM policy, not an inherently read-only DynamoDB service mode. Failover needs fencing of the old writer, reconciliation and an explicit sales-resumption decision.
  • CloudFront/WAF are global account resources; Cognito, SQS and DynamoDB are regional. Edge login arrows show browser identity progression. The audit arrow summarizes a separately configured pipeline, not Lambda writing directly across accounts without permission.
